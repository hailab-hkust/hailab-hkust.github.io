Prof. Zhiming Hu

Lab Director · Tenure-track Assistant Professor

Zhiming Hu leads the Human-centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) Lab at HKUST (Guangzhou), with a joint appointment at HKUST. His research spans VR/AR, HCI, eye tracking, embodied AI, and human-centered AI. He has published 20+ papers at SIGGRAPH, TVCG, IEEE VR, ISMAR, CHI, UIST, and AAAI, including a Best Journal Paper Award at ISMAR 2024. He serves on the TVCG Editorial Board and as an Executive Committee Member of CCF TCVRV.
