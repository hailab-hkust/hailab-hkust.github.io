MPhil Student

Xinya Lu

I’m a first-year MPhil student in Computational Media and Art Thrust of Information Hub at HKUST (GZ), supervised by Professor Zhiming Hu. Previously, I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology in 2025 from Shandong University with Outstanding Graduate Award of Shandong University (6%). My research currently focuses on Interaction-aware Motion In-Betweening, exploring how generative models can synthesize coherent motions from sparse keyframes while preserving realistic human-human interactions. I am also broadly open to exploring new possibilities in human-centered AI.
