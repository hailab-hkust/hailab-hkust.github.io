Visiting Student

Duotun Wang

Duotun Wang joined the HAI Lab as a visiting student in 2025. He obtained a master's degree in computer science at the University of Maryland, College Park, and a bachelor's degree in computer science at the Beijing Institute of Technology. Before coming to HKUST(GZ), He worked as a research engineer at ByteDance and Xiaomi. His main research interests include interaction designs for mixed reality and AI-based visual content creation.
